What you need to know

A GSTIN printed on an invoice should be checked when you are onboarding a supplier, processing an unfamiliar invoice or reviewing a vendor record. The goal is to compare the GSTIN and returned taxpayer information with the invoice—not to treat the GST search as a complete fraud or tax-risk assessment.

How to check it

Compare the result with the invoice issuer's legal or trade name and your vendor master. Also check invoice number, invoice date, place of supply and other invoice fields separately. A GSTIN match does not validate every field on an invoice.

What the result means

First locate the 15-character GSTIN on the invoice. Check that it is in the expected GSTIN format, then use the official GST Portal taxpayer search to look up the number. Review the available name, status and registration information.

Practical review tips

If the GSTIN is active but the name on the search result differs materially from the invoice issuer, request clarification before processing the transaction. Keep the official search result or your verification report with your internal review records where appropriate.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is GSTIN verification the same as GST registration?

No. GST registration is the registration process, while GSTIN is the 15-character identifier associated with a GST registration record.

Is the SarkariSewa India verification service a government service?

No. It is an independent paid service. The official GST Portal remains the government reference for GST records and services.

Can a GSTIN format check prove that the number is active?

No. Format checking can identify obvious structural problems, but actual status and taxpayer details should be checked through the GST taxpayer-search facility.
