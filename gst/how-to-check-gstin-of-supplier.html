What you need to know

A supplier GSTIN check helps you confirm that the GST number supplied by a vendor corresponds with the business details returned by GST search. Use the official GST Portal as the government reference and compare the result with your vendor documents.

How to check it

Start with the official GST Portal taxpayer search. Enter the 15-character GSTIN/UIN and review the available taxpayer details. Compare the legal/trade name and status with the supplier's invoice, quotation, purchase order and other records.

What the result means

Supplier GSTIN checks are useful before onboarding a new vendor, approving a first invoice, changing a supplier master record or investigating a mismatch. A matching GSTIN does not by itself establish that a supplier is commercially reliable.

Practical review tips

If the GSTIN is valid but the returned business details do not match the supplier information, pause the onboarding or invoice workflow and ask the supplier to clarify the discrepancy. Do not edit a GSTIN simply to make it match a document.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is GSTIN verification the same as GST registration?

No. GST registration is the registration process, while GSTIN is the 15-character identifier associated with a GST registration record.

Is the SarkariSewa India verification service a government service?

No. It is an independent paid service. The official GST Portal remains the government reference for GST records and services.

Can a GSTIN format check prove that the number is active?

No. Format checking can identify obvious structural problems, but actual status and taxpayer details should be checked through the GST taxpayer-search facility.
