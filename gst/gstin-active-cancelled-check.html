What you need to know

GSTIN status is an important part of a vendor or invoice review. Before relying on a GST number, check the status returned by the official GST taxpayer-search facility and note the date of your check.

How to check it

An active status is one useful signal when reviewing a GSTIN, but it does not prove that every invoice or transaction is correct. If a GSTIN is cancelled or the status is inconsistent with the supplier's documents, ask the supplier for clarification and consider whether the transaction needs additional tax review.

What the result means

Open the official GST Portal taxpayer search and enter the GSTIN/UIN. Review the status displayed with the taxpayer information. Status can change, so a historical search result should not automatically be treated as the current position.

Practical review tips

For an invoice or supplier decision, record the GSTIN, the status observed, the date checked and the business name shown in the result. This creates a clearer audit trail than relying on an unrecorded verbal confirmation.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is GSTIN verification the same as GST registration?

No. GST registration is the registration process, while GSTIN is the 15-character identifier associated with a GST registration record.

Is the SarkariSewa India verification service a government service?

No. It is an independent paid service. The official GST Portal remains the government reference for GST records and services.

Can a GSTIN format check prove that the number is active?

No. Format checking can identify obvious structural problems, but actual status and taxpayer details should be checked through the GST taxpayer-search facility.
