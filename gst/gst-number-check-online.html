GST number check: what should you compare?

Check the GSTIN itself, legal name, trade name where available, registration date, taxpayer type and status. GSTN identifies these categories among the information available through taxpayer tracking. citeturn0search48

A practical invoice-check workflow

First copy the GSTIN exactly as printed. Second, search it on the GST Portal taxpayer facility. Third, compare the returned business name and status with the invoice. Fourth, investigate any mismatch before treating the invoice as verified. This simple sequence is especially useful for new vendors or manually prepared invoices.

Why GSTIN checks matter for businesses

Incorrect GSTIN data can create problems in invoices, records and tax workflows. GSTN's taxpayer-search material specifically describes verification of GSTIN authenticity and checking GSTINs used on manual or handwritten invoices as use cases. citeturn0search48
