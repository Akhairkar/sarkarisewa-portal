What does GSTIN status mean?

The GST Portal taxpayer-search facility includes GSTIN/UIN status among the information that can be obtained from a taxpayer search. The same GSTN material also lists cancellation date where applicable.

How to check GSTIN status

Use the official GST taxpayer search, choose Search by GSTIN/UIN, enter the complete GSTIN and review the result. Confirm that the returned legal/trade name corresponds with the supplier or business you are dealing with. If a cancellation date or another status is shown, read the result carefully before proceeding.

Active does not mean every transaction is safe

An active GST registration is useful information, but it is not a complete vendor-risk assessment. You should still compare the invoice, business identity, bank/payment details, contract terms and the nature of goods or services. GSTIN status is one compliance check, not a guarantee of a supplier's performance.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where can I check GSTIN status officially?

The GST Portal provides taxpayer-search functionality for GSTIN/UIN and includes status in the information that can be obtained.

Can a GSTIN have a cancellation date?

Yes. GSTN lists cancellation date among the taxpayer details that can be obtained where applicable.

Does active GSTIN status guarantee a genuine supplier?

No. Status is only one part of due diligence. Compare the returned registration information with your invoice and other supplier information.
