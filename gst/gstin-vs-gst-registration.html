GSTIN vs GST Registration

GST registration and GSTIN are related, but they are not the same thing. Learn the difference before checking a business's GST details.

What you need to know

GST registration is the process through which a person or business registers under the GST system. GSTIN is the 15-character identification number associated with a GST registration record. In everyday use, people often use the terms interchangeably, but the distinction matters when reading forms and records.

How to check it

If you are checking a supplier, ask for the GSTIN shown on the relevant invoice or registration documents, then use the official GST Portal search to compare the returned details. Do not assume that possession of a GSTIN alone proves that every transaction is tax-compliant.

What the result means

Think of registration as the government registration process and GSTIN as the identifier used to identify the registered taxpayer or registration record. A business may have GST-related records that need to be reviewed by state, registration and status rather than by the number alone.

Practical review tips

For businesses, GST registration questions and GSTIN verification are different tasks. Registration guidance should use official GST resources; verification should focus on the actual GSTIN and the taxpayer details returned for it.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is GSTIN verification the same as GST registration?

No. GST registration is the registration process, while GSTIN is the 15-character identifier associated with a GST registration record.

Is the SarkariSewa India verification service a government service?

No. It is an independent paid service. The official GST Portal remains the government reference for GST records and services.

Can a GSTIN format check prove that the number is active?

No. Format checking can identify obvious structural problems, but actual status and taxpayer details should be checked through the GST taxpayer-search facility.
