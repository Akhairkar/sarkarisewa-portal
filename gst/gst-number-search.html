GST Number Search Online

Search a GST number and understand the details returned by GST taxpayer search.

Searching a GST number is commonly needed when checking an invoice, identifying a supplier's registration details or confirming that a GSTIN belongs to the business you are dealing with. This guide explains what a GST number search can tell you and where the independent ₹20 verification service fits.

What is a GST number search?

GST number search means looking up a GSTIN/UIN in the taxpayer-search facility to retrieve available registration information. GSTN's taxpayer-search material states that users can search by GSTIN/UIN/PAN and obtain information such as legal name, trade name, jurisdiction, registration date, constitution, taxpayer type and status. citeturn0search48

How to search a GST number

Start with the complete 15-character GSTIN printed on the invoice, website, quotation or vendor document. Open the official GST Portal taxpayer search, select the GSTIN/UIN search option, enter the number and complete any verification requested by the portal. Review the returned details rather than checking only whether the number has the expected length.

GST number search for invoices

When an invoice is received from a new supplier, compare the GSTIN returned by the search with the GSTIN printed on the invoice and the business name shown on the document. GSTN specifically lists verification of GSTINs appearing on manual or handwritten invoices as one use of taxpayer search. citeturn0search48

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I search a GST number online?

Yes. GSTN provides taxpayer-search functionality through the GST Portal. citeturn0search48

What should I compare after a GST number search?

Compare the GSTIN, legal/trade name, registration status and other returned information with the supplier's invoice or business documents.
