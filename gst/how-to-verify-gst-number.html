How to Verify a GST Number Online

A practical step-by-step GSTIN verification guide for invoices and suppliers.

If a supplier gives you a GST number, the safest first step is to check it through the official GST taxpayer-search facility and compare the result with the documents you received. This guide walks through that process and explains the optional SarkariSewa India ₹20 report.

Step 1: Get the correct GSTIN

Use the GSTIN exactly as shown on the invoice, quotation, purchase order or vendor record. A GSTIN is a 15-character identifier, so copy it carefully rather than relying on a partial number.

Step 2: Use the official GST taxpayer search

Open the GST Portal's taxpayer search and select the GSTIN/UIN search option. Enter the GSTIN and complete the portal's verification step. GSTN's own guidance confirms that taxpayer search can be used to verify GSTIN authenticity and retrieve registration information. citeturn0search48

Step 3: Compare the result

Compare the legal name and trade name, if available, with the supplier information. Also review registration date, constitution, taxpayer type and GSTIN status. GSTN lists these fields among the details available through taxpayer tracking. citeturn0search48

Step 4: Investigate mismatches

If the GSTIN result does not match the invoice or vendor record, stop and clarify the difference before making a payment or using the GSTIN in a return. A mismatch can be a simple typing error, but it can also indicate that the wrong registration has been supplied.

Step 5: Use a structured ₹20 report when needed

If you want the verification information in a clean report-oriented format, use SarkariSewa India's independent ₹20 verification service. It is a convenience/report workflow based on an authorized provider response; it is not a government certificate and does not replace the GST Portal.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I verify a GST number?

Use the GST Portal taxpayer search, select Search by GSTIN/UIN, enter the GSTIN and review the returned registration details. citeturn0search48

Which GST details should I compare with an invoice?

Compare the GSTIN, legal/trade name, status and other available registration information with the supplier's invoice and business documents.

Can I use SarkariSewa India instead of the official GST Portal?

The official portal remains the government reference. SarkariSewa India's ₹20 service is an independent report-oriented verification workflow and should not be represented as the government portal.
