Why verify a GSTIN before doing business?

A GSTIN is a 15-character identification number associated with a registered person under GST. Checking the number and the returned taxpayer details can help you compare the GSTIN with the business name shown on an invoice or vendor record. GSTN's taxpayer-search guidance lists information such as legal name, trade name, registration date, constitution, taxpayer type and GSTIN status among the details that can be obtained through taxpayer search. citeturn0search48

What can you check?

Depending on the response available through the verification route, you may be able to review the GSTIN/UIN status, legal name, trade name, registration date, taxpayer type, business constitution and other available business information. Do not treat a search result as proof of the commercial quality of a supplier; use it as one verification step alongside invoices, contracts and other due diligence.

Official GST portal method

For an official self-check, use the GST Portal's taxpayer search and choose the GSTIN/UIN search option. Enter the 15-character GSTIN and review the details returned by the portal.
