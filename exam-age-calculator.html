Exam Age Eligibility Calculator

Apni exact age nikalein aur ek click mein dekhein ki aap SSC, UPSC, Railway, Banking jaise 11+ major exams ke liye age-wise eligible hain ya nahi — category relaxation (OBC/SC/ST/PwD) ke saath.

Details bharkar apni age aur 11 exams ke liye eligibility dekhein

Yeh Kaise Kaam Karta Hai

Har government exam notification mein ek "age as on" cut-off date di jaati hai — jaise UPSC Civil Services ke liye 1 August, ya SSC exams ke liye notification mein di gayi specific date. Aapki age is exact date tak, saal-mahine-din mein calculate hoti hai — form bharne ke din tak nahi. Ek din ka farak bhi eligibility decide kar sakta hai, isliye manual calculation mein galti hona aam baat hai.

Yeh tool aapki date of birth aur cut-off date ke beech ka exact gap nikalta hai, phir usse 11 major exams ki general age-limit range se compare karta hai — aapki category ke hisaab se relaxation add karke. Result turant, bina kisi login ya document upload ke milta hai.

FAQ

Exam age eligibility ke liye "as on" date kya hoti hai?

Har exam notification mein ek fixed cut-off date di jaati hai (jaise 1 August ya 1 January) — aapki age us din tak calculate hoti hai, form bharne ke din tak nahi. Yeh date har saal aur har exam ke liye alag ho sakti hai, isliye hamesha official notification se cut-off date confirm karein.

Category relaxation kitni milti hai?

Zyadatar central government exams mein OBC (non-creamy layer) ko 3 saal, SC/ST ko 5 saal, aur PwD candidates ko 10 saal ki upper-age relaxation milti hai General category ki upper limit se upar. Kuch exams mein ex-servicemen ke liye alag relaxation bhi hoti hai — is calculator mein woh cover nahi hai, official notification check karein.

Kya yeh calculator official hai?

Nahi, yeh ek independent quick-check tool hai jo publicly published general age-limit guidelines par based hai. Final eligibility hamesha us saal ke official exam notification (SSC, UPSC, RRB, IBPS, ya state PSC ki website) se hi confirm karein, kyunki age limits saal-dar-saal aur post-wise badal sakte hain.
