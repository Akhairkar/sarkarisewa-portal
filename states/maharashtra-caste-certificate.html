Maharashtra Caste Certificate

A documentary proof of a person's belonging to a particular caste, as specified in the Indian Constitution.

Overview: Caste Certificate in Maharashtra

Official Portal Aaple Sarkar
Application Fee ₹33.60
Processing Time 45 Days
Issuing Authority SDO (Sub-Divisional Officer)
Validity Lifetime
Visit Official Portal →

Documents Required

To apply for a Caste Certificate in Maharashtra, you generally need the following documents:

  • Identity Proof
  • Address Proof
  • Extract of birth register of applicant/father/grandfather
  • Caste proof of relatives

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What is the application fee for Caste Certificate in Maharashtra?

The application fee is generally ₹33.60 when applying through the official Aaple Sarkar portal.

How many days does it take to get a Caste Certificate in Maharashtra?

The standard processing time is 45 Days. However, it may take longer depending on verification by the SDO (Sub-Divisional Officer).

What is the validity of the Caste Certificate?

The Caste Certificate issued by the Maharashtra government has a validity of Lifetime.

Need Help?

If you have all the documents ready but aren't sure how to proceed, visit the official Aaple Sarkar portal or your nearest Common Service Center (CSC) / e-Mitra in Maharashtra.
