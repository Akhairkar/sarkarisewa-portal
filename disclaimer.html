Disclaimer

Please read this disclaimer carefully before using SarkariSewa Portal.

1. Not a government website

SarkariSewa Portal is an independent, privately-run information guide. We are not affiliated with, endorsed by, or an official representative of the Government of India or any state government, ministry, department or public sector body.

2. Informational purpose only

All content — service descriptions, eligibility criteria, fees, timelines, documents required and FAQs — is provided for general informational purposes only and is compiled from publicly available government sources.

3. No application processing

We do not accept applications, collect documents, charge government fees, or process any government service on your behalf. Every "Apply Online", "Download Form" and "Track Status" link takes you directly to the relevant official government website to complete your task there.

4. Accuracy & changes

Government rules, fees, forms, portals and helpline numbers change from time to time, sometimes without notice. While we try to keep this portal accurate and up to date, we cannot guarantee that every detail is current. Always verify critical details (fees, deadlines, required documents) on the linked official website before acting.

5. No liability

We are not responsible for any loss, delay, rejection or damage arising from reliance on information found on this portal, or from your use of any third-party government website linked from here.

6. External links

This portal links to official government websites and, where the exact application portal varies by state, to national directory portals. We do not control and are not responsible for the content, availability or security of any external website.

7. Contact

If you spot outdated or incorrect information, please let us know via the contact page linked in the footer so we can correct it.
