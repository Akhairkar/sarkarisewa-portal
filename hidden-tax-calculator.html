Yeh Calculator Kaise Kaam Karta Hai

Bharat me har purchase — chahe petrol ho ya grocery, mobile recharge ho ya shopping — uski price ke andar hi indirect tax (GST, excise duty, ya state VAT) chhupa hota hai. Yeh direct income tax se alag hai; ismein aapko koi separate bill nahi milta jo bataye ki kitna tax gaya, isliye zyadatar log ise notice hi nahi karte.

Yeh calculator har category ke liye ek approximate tax-rate range use karta hai (jaise petrol ~45-55%, mobile/OTT 18% flat GST, alcohol 40-60% state excise ke saath) aur aapki bhari hui monthly amount par apply karke ek estimated range deta hai — exact figure nahi, kyunki real-world rates state, brand aur product ke hisaab se badalte hain.

FAQ

Hidden tax kya hota hai?

Jab aap petrol, grocery, mobile recharge ya shopping karte hain, toh price ke andar hi GST, excise duty ya VAT chhupa hota hai — aapko alag se dikhta nahi, lekin aap wo tax har purchase par chuka rahe hote hain.

Yeh calculator kitna accurate hai?

Yeh ek estimated range deta hai, exact figure nahi — kyunki actual tax product, brand, state aur applicable GST slab ke hisaab se alag hota hai. Yeh tool sirf general awareness ke liye hai, financial ya tax advice nahi.

Petrol par itna zyada tax kyun hai?

Petrol par central excise duty aur state VAT dono lagte hain, jo combined milakar base price ka 45-55% tak ho sakta hai — state ke hisaab se rate alag hota hai.
