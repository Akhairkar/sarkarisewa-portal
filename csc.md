# CSC Locator Pages Tracking

## Overview
Based on keyword research, "CSC center near me", "Common service center", and localized terms like "Maha e Seva Kendra" have high transactional intent for Aadhaar updates, e-Shram, and banking services. 
To avoid "thin content" penalties and test traffic, we are limiting generation to:
1. **36 State Pages** (Completed)
2. **Top 100 Demanding Cities/Districts** (To-Do)

## SEO Title Strategy (Bilingual & Unique)
To prevent duplicate title penalties, titles will use varied templates:
- `Lakhs of CSC Centers Directory: Find Nearest e-Seva in [City]`
- `[City] CSC Locator: Aadhaar & Common Service Centers Near Me`
- `Maha e-Seva & CSC Kendra in [City], [State] - Full List`
- `सीएससी केंद्र [City]: Find Nearest Common Service Center`
- `[City] में अपना नजदीकी CSC / ग्राहक सेवा केंद्र खोजें`

## Page Architecture (Anti-Thin Content)
Every page MUST include:
- `Header & Footer` (Properly linked)
- `Related Tools & Services Icons` (Cross-linking to ITR, Pan Card, etc.)
- `Dynamic FAQs` (Specific to the city/state)
- `Breadcrumbs` (Home > CSC Locator > State > City)
