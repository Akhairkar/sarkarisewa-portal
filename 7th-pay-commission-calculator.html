7th Pay Commission In-Hand Salary Components Explained

1. Dearness Allowance (DA) Calculation Formula

Dearness allowance central govt employees ko inflation se protect karne ke liye saal me do baar (January & July) modify kiya jata hai. Calculation formula:

DA Amount = Basic Pay × (Current DA Percentage / 100)

2. House Rent Allowance (HRA) Revision Rules

7th Pay Commission guidelines ke mutabiq, jab DA 50% cross kar jata hai, tab HRA revised percentage par lagta hai:

  • X Class Cities (Metro): 30% of Basic Pay (Pahle 24% tha)
  • Y Class Cities (Tier-2): 20% of Basic Pay (Pahle 16% tha)
  • Z Class Cities (Tier-3/Rural): 10% of Basic Pay (Pahle 8% tha)

3. Transport Allowance (TA) & DA on TA (TATP) Rules

Transport allowance monthly travel cost ke liye diya jata hai. Higher TPTA cities ke liye Pay Level 9 aur usse upar Rs. 7200 + DA milta hai, jabki lower levels ke liye Rs. 3600 ya Rs. 1350 hota hai.

4. NPS (National Pension System) Employee Deduction

Har Govt Employee ki monthly basic pay aur DA ka 10% NPS me deduct hota hai, aur Government usme 14% apni taraf se PRAN account me accumulate karti hai.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Level 7 (Grade Pay ₹4600) ka starting Basic Pay ₹44,900 hota hai. 50% DA, X Class HRA (30%) aur TA add karne par Gross salary lagbhag ₹85,000+ banti hai. NPS 10% deduction ke baad net in-hand salary approximate ₹75,000 to ₹78,000 milti hai.

TATP ka full form 'Dearness Allowance on Transport Allowance' hai. Transport Allowance (TA) par jo current DA percentage chala hota hai use TATP kehte hain. For example: Agar TA ₹3,600 hai aur DA 50% hai, toh TATP = ₹3,600 × 50% = ₹1,800 hoga.

Haan, agar aapko Government Accommodation / Quarter allotted hai toh aapko House Rent Allowance (HRA) nahi milega, balki Licence Fee deduct ho sakti hai.
