Common Reasons for Linking Failure

  • Initials vs Full Name: Aadhaar has "R. Sharma" but PAN has "Rahul Sharma". This is a mismatch and will fail. You must expand the initials in Aadhaar or shorten them in PAN to match exactly.
  • Only Year of Birth in Aadhaar: Old Aadhaar cards only printed the Year of Birth (e.g., 1985) instead of the full DOB (e.g., 12/04/1985). You must update your Aadhaar to show the full DOB before linking.
  • Challan 280 Confusion: The ₹1000 penalty MUST be paid under "Major Head 0021" and "Minor Head 500". Any other combination will result in payment failure and linking will not proceed.
