Frequently Asked Questions

Are Generic Medicines as safe as Branded ones?

Yes! Generic medicines sold at Jan Aushadhi Kendras have the exact same active ingredients, dosage, safety, and quality as expensive branded drugs. They are certified by WHO-GMP.

Why are Jan Aushadhi medicines so cheap?

Branded companies spend crores of rupees on marketing, medical representatives, and packaging. Jan Aushadhi removes all these marketing costs, providing the medicine directly from the manufacturer to the patient.

Where can I buy these cheap medicines?

You can buy them at any of the 10,000+ PMBJK (Pradhan Mantri Bhartiya Janaushadhi Pariyojana Kendra) across India. Use our Jan Aushadhi Store Locator to find the nearest medical store in your district.
