Quick Overview

The National Pension System (NPS) is a voluntary, long-term retirement savings scheme regulated by the Pension Fund Regulatory and Development Authority (PFRDA) of India. Launched in 2004 for government employees and opened to all citizens in 2009, NPS offers market-linked returns with professional fund management. It provides significant tax benefits under Section 80CCD(1) and 80CCD(1B) of the Income Tax Act, making it one of India's most tax-efficient retirement planning instruments. NPS has two account types: Tier-1 (mandatory pension account with withdrawal restrictions) and Tier-2 (voluntary savings account with flexible withdrawals).

Eligibility Criteria

  • Any Indian citizen (resident or non-resident) aged 18–70 years can open NPS
  • Must have a valid KYC (Aadhaar/PAN)
  • NRIs can open NPS accounts but must comply with FEMA regulations
  • Government employees (Central and State) are mandatorily covered under NPS

Documents Required

  • PAN Card (mandatory)
  • Aadhaar Card (for eKYC or offline KYC)
  • Bank account details with IFSC code
  • Passport size photograph
  • Cancelled cheque or bank statement
  • Mobile number and email ID

Step-by-Step NPS Account Opening Process

  1. Visit enps.nsdl.com or cra-nsdl.com
  2. Click on 'Registration' → Select 'Individual Subscriber'
  3. Choose between eKYC (Aadhaar OTP) or PAN-based KYC
  4. Fill in personal details, address, nominee information
  5. Select Pension Fund Manager (SBI, LIC, HDFC, ICICI, Kotak, UTI, Axis, Max Life, Tata)
  6. Choose your investment allocation (Auto Choice or Active Choice)
  7. Make minimum initial contribution: ₹500 for Tier-1, ₹1,000 for Tier-2
  8. Complete payment via net banking/UPI
  9. Receive your PRAN (Permanent Retirement Account Number) via SMS/email

Tax Benefits & Fees

Tax Benefits: Deduction up to ₹1.5 lakh under Section 80CCD(1) + additional ₹50,000 under 80CCD(1B) = Total ₹2 lakh per year. Fund Management Fees: Very low – 0.01%–0.09% per annum. Account opening is free online.

How to Check NPS Account Status & Balance

  • Login to cra-nsdl.com using PRAN and password
  • NSDL NPS Mobile App
  • Call PFRDA Toll-Free: 1800-110-708
  • SMS: NPSBAL PRAN to 9999

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the minimum contribution for NPS Tier-1 account?

Minimum ₹500 per contribution and ₹1,000 per year for Tier-1 account. There is no maximum limit. For Tier-2, minimum ₹250 per contribution.

Can I withdraw from NPS before retirement (age 60)?

Partial withdrawal (up to 25% of own contribution) is allowed after 3 years for specific purposes like higher education, marriage, home purchase, or critical illness treatment.

What happens to NPS corpus at retirement (age 60)?

At 60, you must use at least 40% of the corpus to purchase an annuity (monthly pension) from an empaneled insurer. The remaining 60% can be withdrawn as a lump sum, tax-free.

Which fund manager is best for NPS?

Performance varies year to year. SBI Pension Funds, LIC Pension Fund, and HDFC Pension Fund are consistently among the top performers. Compare current returns on the NPS Trust website.

Is NPS better than PPF for tax saving?

NPS offers higher potential returns (8–12% market-linked) vs PPF (7.1% fixed), with an extra ₹50,000 deduction under 80CCD(1B). However, PPF offers full liquidity and EEE tax status. Both are complementary retirement tools.

Can NRIs open an NPS account?

Yes, NRIs (Non-Resident Indians) aged 18–70 can open NPS Tier-1 accounts. However, the NPS account will be closed if the subscriber becomes an OCI/PIO. FEMA regulations apply for contributions.

संक्षिप्त परिचय

राष्ट्रीय पेंशन प्रणाली (NPS) PFRDA द्वारा विनियमित एक स्वैच्छिक दीर्घकालिक सेवानिवृत्ति बचत योजना है। 2004 में सरकारी कर्मचारियों के लिए और 2009 में सभी नागरिकों के लिए शुरू की गई। NPS बाजार से जुड़े रिटर्न और धारा 80CCD में ₹2 लाख तक टैक्स लाभ देती है।

पात्रता मानदंड

  • 18–70 वर्ष का कोई भी भारतीय नागरिक (निवासी या अनिवासी)
  • वैध KYC (आधार/PAN) अनिवार्य
  • NRI भी FEMA नियमों के अनुसार आवेदन कर सकते हैं

आवश्यक दस्तावेज़

  • PAN कार्ड (अनिवार्य)
  • आधार कार्ड
  • IFSC के साथ बैंक खाता विवरण
  • पासपोर्ट साइज फोटो
  • मोबाइल और ईमेल

NPS खाता खोलने की प्रक्रिया

  1. enps.nsdl.com पर जाएं
  2. 'Registration' → 'Individual Subscriber' चुनें
  3. eKYC (आधार OTP) या PAN-based KYC चुनें
  4. व्यक्तिगत विवरण, पता, नामांकित व्यक्ति की जानकारी भरें
  5. Pension Fund Manager चुनें
  6. Tier-1 के लिए न्यूनतम ₹500 जमा करें
  7. PRAN (Permanent Retirement Account Number) SMS/ईमेल पर मिलेगा

टैक्स लाभ और शुल्क

धारा 80CCD(1) में ₹1.5 लाख + 80CCD(1B) में अतिरिक्त ₹50,000 = कुल ₹2 लाख/वर्ष टैक्स कटौती। Fund Management शुल्क मात्र 0.01%–0.09%।

अक्सर पूछे जाने वाले प्रश्न

NPS Tier-1 में न्यूनतम योगदान कितना है?

प्रति योगदान न्यूनतम ₹500 और प्रति वर्ष ₹1,000।

रिटायरमेंट से पहले NPS से पैसे निकाल सकते हैं?

3 साल बाद उच्च शिक्षा, विवाह, गृह खरीद या गंभीर बीमारी के लिए स्वयं के योगदान का 25% तक निकाल सकते हैं।

60 वर्ष पर NPS corpus का क्या होता है?

40% से वार्षिकी (मासिक पेंशन) खरीदनी होती है; शेष 60% कर-मुक्त एकमुश्त निकाल सकते हैं।
