Quick Overview

PAN (Permanent Account Number) is a 10-digit alphanumeric identifier issued by the Income Tax Department of India. It is mandatory for all financial transactions above specified limits, filing income tax returns, opening bank accounts, and various other financial activities. There are multiple ways to apply for a PAN card in India: through the NSDL (Protean) portal, UTI Infrastructure portal, or by obtaining an Instant e-PAN using Aadhaar (free of cost for new applicants).

Types of PAN Application

  • New PAN: First-time applicants (Form 49A for Indian citizens, Form 49AA for foreign entities)
  • PAN Correction/Update: Change of name, date of birth, address, or other details
  • Duplicate PAN: If original is lost/damaged
  • Instant e-PAN: Via Aadhaar – free, immediate issuance (only for new applicants)

Documents Required (Indian Citizen)

  • Identity Proof: Aadhaar / Voter ID / Passport / Driving License
  • Address Proof: Aadhaar / Utility bill / Bank statement
  • Date of Birth proof: Aadhaar / Birth Certificate / School leaving certificate
  • Passport size photograph

Step-by-Step Online Application Process

  1. Option 1 (NSDL/Protean): Visit onlineservices.nsdl.com → New PAN – Indian Citizen (Form 49A) → Fill details → Upload documents → Pay ₹107 (India delivery) → Submit
  2. Option 2 (UTI): Visit utiitsl.com → Apply for PAN → Same process
  3. Option 3 (Instant e-PAN): Visit www.incometax.gov.in → 'Instant e-PAN' → Enter Aadhaar → OTP verification → e-PAN issued free immediately (no physical card)
  4. Note Application Number/Acknowledgement Number
  5. Physical PAN card dispatched within 15–20 days (for postal applications)

Fees

Indian address: ₹107 (₹93 + 15% GST). Foreign address: ₹1,017. Instant e-PAN via Aadhaar: Free. Correction/Reprint: ₹107 (India) / ₹1,017 (abroad).

PAN Status Check

  • NSDL: tin.tin.nsdl.com → Track PAN Status
  • UTI: utiitsl.com → PAN Status
  • Income Tax Portal: incometax.gov.in → Know Your PAN
  • SMS: NSDLPAN 15-digit Acknowledgement No. to 57575

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I get instant PAN card online free?

Yes! The Instant e-PAN facility on the Income Tax portal (incometax.gov.in) allows Aadhaar holders to get a free e-PAN within minutes. It's valid and accepted everywhere. Physical card can be obtained later by paying ₹50.

Is PAN card mandatory for a savings bank account?

Yes, PAN (or Form 60 declaration if no PAN) is required to open a savings bank account. PAN is also mandatory for deposits/withdrawals above ₹50,000 in cash.

Can I have two PAN cards?

No. Holding two PAN cards is illegal and can lead to a penalty of ₹10,000 under Section 272B of the Income Tax Act. If you have two, surrender one immediately at an NSDL/UTI center or online.

How to link PAN with Aadhaar?

Visit incometax.gov.in → Link Aadhaar → Enter PAN and Aadhaar → Pay ₹1,000 fee (deadline passed, fee applicable now). PAN becomes inoperative if not linked.

संक्षिप्त परिचय

PAN (Permanent Account Number) आयकर विभाग द्वारा जारी 10 अंकों का अक्षरांकीय पहचान कोड है। यह ITR फाइलिंग, बैंक खाता खोलने और बड़े वित्तीय लेनदेन के लिए अनिवार्य है। NSDL, UTI या आधार से Instant e-PAN के जरिए आवेदन कर सकते हैं।

आवश्यक दस्तावेज़

  • पहचान प्रमाण: आधार / वोटर ID / पासपोर्ट
  • पता प्रमाण: आधार / बिल / बैंक स्टेटमेंट
  • जन्म तिथि प्रमाण: आधार / जन्म प्रमाण पत्र
  • पासपोर्ट साइज फोटो

ऑनलाइन आवेदन प्रक्रिया

  1. विकल्प 1 (NSDL): onlineservices.nsdl.com → Form 49A भरें → ₹107 भुगतान
  2. विकल्प 2 (Instant e-PAN): incometax.gov.in → 'Instant e-PAN' → आधार OTP → तुरंत मुफ्त
  3. 15-20 दिन में भौतिक कार्ड डाक से मिलेगा

अक्सर पूछे जाने वाले प्रश्न

क्या PAN कार्ड मुफ्त में ऑनलाइन मिल सकता है?

हाँ! incometax.gov.in पर Instant e-PAN आधार धारकों को मुफ्त में मिनटों में मिलता है।

क्या दो PAN कार्ड रख सकते हैं?

नहीं, दो PAN रखना अवैध है और ₹10,000 जुर्माना हो सकता है।
