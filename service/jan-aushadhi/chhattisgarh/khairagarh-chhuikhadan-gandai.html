Khairagarh Chhuikhadan Gandai mein kitne Jan Aushadhi Kendra hain?

Khairagarh Chhuikhadan Gandai, Chhattisgarh mein kul 7 Pradhan Mantri Bhartiya Janaushadhi Pariyojana (PMBJP) stores hain jahan aapko 1900+ generic medicines 50-90% discount par mil sakti hain.

Kya Jan Aushadhi ki dawaiyaan safe hain?

Haan, sabhi generic medicines WHO-GMP certified laboratories se aati hain aur CDSCO dwara approved hain. Quality branded dawai jaisi hi hoti hai, sirf price kam hoti hai kyunki marketing cost nahi hota.

Khairagarh Chhuikhadan Gandai mein Jan Aushadhi Kendra kaise kholen?
