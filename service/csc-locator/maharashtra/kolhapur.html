Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What services are available at a CSC center in Kolhapur?

Centers in Kolhapur offer Aadhaar updates, PAN card applications, passport services, banking, e-Shram registration, and state-specific certificate (income/caste) applications.

Q: How do I find the WhatsApp number of a CSC VLE in Kolhapur?

Simply click on the 'WhatsApp' button on the verified center cards below. If a center in Kolhapur is claimed by the owner, their direct contact is unlocked.

Q: What are the standard timings for CSCs in Kolhapur?

Most centers in Kolhapur operate from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, Monday to Saturday. However, some independent VLEs may have extended timings.
