Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How do I find the WhatsApp number of a CSC VLE in Jalgaon?

Simply click on the 'WhatsApp' button on the verified center cards below. If a center in Jalgaon is claimed by the owner, their direct contact is unlocked.

Q: Can I update my Aadhaar mobile number at any Jalgaon CSC?

Not all centers have Aadhaar update machines. It is best to WhatsApp or call the specific Jalgaon center from our list to confirm before visiting.

Q: What are the standard timings for CSCs in Jalgaon?

Most centers in Jalgaon operate from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, Monday to Saturday. However, some independent VLEs may have extended timings.
