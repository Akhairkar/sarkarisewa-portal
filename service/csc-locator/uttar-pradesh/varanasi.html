Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How do I find the WhatsApp number of a CSC VLE in Varanasi?

Simply click on the 'WhatsApp' button on the verified center cards below. If a center in Varanasi is claimed by the owner, their direct contact is unlocked.

Q: What services are available at a CSC center in Varanasi?

Centers in Varanasi offer Aadhaar updates, PAN card applications, passport services, banking, e-Shram registration, and state-specific certificate (income/caste) applications.

Q: Is there any fee to check CSC locations in Varanasi?

No, using our SarkariSewaIndia directory to find CSC centers in Varanasi is 100% free.
