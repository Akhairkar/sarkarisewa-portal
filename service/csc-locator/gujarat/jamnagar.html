Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Is there any fee to check CSC locations in Jamnagar?

No, using our SarkariSewaIndia directory to find CSC centers in Jamnagar is 100% free.

Q: Can I update my Aadhaar mobile number at any Jamnagar CSC?

Not all centers have Aadhaar update machines. It is best to WhatsApp or call the specific Jamnagar center from our list to confirm before visiting.

Q: What are the standard timings for CSCs in Jamnagar?

Most centers in Jamnagar operate from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, Monday to Saturday. However, some independent VLEs may have extended timings.
