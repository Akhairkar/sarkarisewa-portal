Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Can I update my Aadhaar mobile number at any Kakinada CSC?

Not all centers have Aadhaar update machines. It is best to WhatsApp or call the specific Kakinada center from our list to confirm before visiting.

Q: How do I find the WhatsApp number of a CSC VLE in Kakinada?

Simply click on the 'WhatsApp' button on the verified center cards below. If a center in Kakinada is claimed by the owner, their direct contact is unlocked.

Q: Are Maha e-Seva Kendras and CSCs the same in Andhra Pradesh?

Yes, in Andhra Pradesh, CSCs are often referred to as Maha e-Seva Kendras or Jan Seva Kendras, providing identical Digital India government services.
