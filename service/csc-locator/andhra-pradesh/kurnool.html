Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Are Maha e-Seva Kendras and CSCs the same in Andhra Pradesh?

Yes, in Andhra Pradesh, CSCs are often referred to as Maha e-Seva Kendras or Jan Seva Kendras, providing identical Digital India government services.

Q: What services are available at a CSC center in Kurnool?

Centers in Kurnool offer Aadhaar updates, PAN card applications, passport services, banking, e-Shram registration, and state-specific certificate (income/caste) applications.

Q: Can I update my Aadhaar mobile number at any Kurnool CSC?

Not all centers have Aadhaar update machines. It is best to WhatsApp or call the specific Kurnool center from our list to confirm before visiting.
