Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What are the standard timings for CSCs in Bilaspur?

Most centers in Bilaspur operate from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, Monday to Saturday. However, some independent VLEs may have extended timings.

Q: Is there any fee to check CSC locations in Bilaspur?

No, using our SarkariSewaIndia directory to find CSC centers in Bilaspur is 100% free.

Q: Are Maha e-Seva Kendras and CSCs the same in Chhattisgarh?

Yes, in Chhattisgarh, CSCs are often referred to as Maha e-Seva Kendras or Jan Seva Kendras, providing identical Digital India government services.
