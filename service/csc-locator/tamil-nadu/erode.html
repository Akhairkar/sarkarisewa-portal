Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What services are available at a CSC center in Erode?

Centers in Erode offer Aadhaar updates, PAN card applications, passport services, banking, e-Shram registration, and state-specific certificate (income/caste) applications.

Q: What are the standard timings for CSCs in Erode?

Most centers in Erode operate from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, Monday to Saturday. However, some independent VLEs may have extended timings.

Q: Is there any fee to check CSC locations in Erode?

No, using our SarkariSewaIndia directory to find CSC centers in Erode is 100% free.
