Quick Overview

The National Apprenticeship Promotion Scheme (NAPS) is a flagship skill development initiative by the Ministry of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship, Government of India. The scheme promotes apprenticeship training by incentivizing employers to engage apprentices and sharing the cost of stipend paid to apprentices. Under NAPS, 25% of the stipend (up to ₹1,500 per month) is reimbursed to employers by the Government, encouraging more industries to hire apprentices. This creates a win-win situation for both industry and youth seeking practical work experience.

Eligibility Criteria

  • For Apprentices: Age 14–35 years; educational qualification as per the trade (8th pass to Graduate level)
  • Fresher looking for skill-based practical training in industry
  • ITI, Diploma, Graduate, or Post-Graduate holders eligible based on the trade
  • For Employers: Any establishment with 30 or more employees must mandatorily engage apprentices (0.5%–15% of workforce)
  • Small establishments with fewer employees can also voluntarily register

Documents Required (Apprentice)

  • Aadhaar Card
  • Date of Birth proof (Birth Certificate / 10th Marksheet)
  • Educational certificates (ITI/Diploma/Degree as applicable)
  • Passport size photograph
  • Bank account details
  • Mobile number (for OTP registration)

Step-by-Step Online Registration Process

  1. Visit apprenticeshipindia.org and click on 'Register as Apprentice'
  2. Enter your mobile number and verify via OTP
  3. Fill in personal details: name, Aadhaar, date of birth, address
  4. Upload educational certificates and Aadhaar
  5. Select your preferred trade/sector and location
  6. Browse available apprenticeship vacancies and apply to suitable employers
  7. Employer will review your profile and send an offer/invitation
  8. Accept the offer and sign the apprenticeship contract online
  9. Report to the employer on the joining date and begin training
  10. Stipend is credited monthly to your bank account

Fees & Stipend

Registration on apprenticeshipindia.org is completely free. Apprentices receive a monthly stipend based on their educational qualification: ITI pass – ₹7,000–₹9,000; Diploma – ₹8,000–₹10,000; Graduate – ₹9,000–₹12,000. The Government reimburses 25% of prescribed stipend (max ₹1,500/month) to employers.

How to Check Application Status

  • Login to apprenticeshipindia.org → Dashboard → Application Status
  • Check email/SMS for employer responses
  • Contact the Regional Apprenticeship Directorate for assistance
  • Helpline: 1800-123-9626 (Skill India)

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between apprenticeship and internship?

Apprenticeship under NAPS is a legally binding training program under the Apprentices Act 1961, with a mandatory stipend, government oversight, and a National Apprenticeship Certificate upon completion. Internships are informal arrangements with no such guarantees.

Can a fresher with no work experience apply for NAPS?

Yes, NAPS is specifically designed for freshers and youth seeking their first practical work experience in industry. No prior work experience is required.

How long does an apprenticeship last under NAPS?

Apprenticeship duration ranges from 6 months to 3 years depending on the trade, qualification level, and employer requirements.

Is NAPS apprenticeship stipend taxable?

Apprenticeship stipend is generally exempt from income tax as it is considered a training allowance, not salary income. However, consult a tax professional for specific advice.

Can I do apprenticeship while pursuing regular college education?

NAPS apprenticeship is primarily for those who have completed their education. However, students in their final year or those pursuing distance/correspondence courses may be eligible in some cases.

What certificate do I get after completing apprenticeship?

Upon successful completion, apprentices receive a National Apprenticeship Certificate (NAC) issued by the NCVT (National Council for Vocational Training), which is recognized across India and adds significant value to your resume.

संक्षिप्त परिचय

राष्ट्रीय शिक्षुता प्रोत्साहन योजना (NAPS) कौशल विकास और उद्यमिता मंत्रालय की एक प्रमुख योजना है। इस योजना में सरकार नियोक्ताओं को शिक्षुता में लगे युवाओं को दिए जाने वाले वजीफे का 25% (अधिकतम ₹1,500/माह) प्रतिपूर्ति करती है। इससे उद्योग और युवा दोनों को फायदा होता है।

पात्रता मानदंड

  • शिक्षार्थी के लिए: आयु 14–35 वर्ष; ट्रेड के अनुसार 8वीं से स्नातक तक
  • नियोक्ता के लिए: 30 या अधिक कर्मचारियों वाले संस्थानों के लिए शिक्षुता अनिवार्य

आवश्यक दस्तावेज़

  • आधार कार्ड
  • जन्म तिथि प्रमाण
  • शैक्षिक प्रमाण पत्र (ITI/डिप्लोमा/डिग्री)
  • पासपोर्ट साइज फोटो
  • बैंक खाता विवरण
  • मोबाइल नंबर

ऑनलाइन पंजीकरण प्रक्रिया

  1. apprenticeshipindia.org पर जाएं और 'Register as Apprentice' पर क्लिक करें
  2. मोबाइल नंबर OTP से वेरीफाई करें
  3. व्यक्तिगत विवरण और शैक्षिक दस्तावेज़ भरें व अपलोड करें
  4. पसंदीदा ट्रेड और स्थान चुनें
  5. उपलब्ध रिक्तियों में आवेदन करें
  6. नियोक्ता से ऑफर मिलने पर अनुबंध साइन करें
  7. नियत तिथि पर रिपोर्ट करें और प्रशिक्षण शुरू करें

वजीफा और शुल्क

पंजीकरण पूरी तरह मुफ्त। ITI उत्तीर्ण: ₹7,000–₹9,000/माह; डिप्लोमा: ₹8,000–₹10,000/माह; स्नातक: ₹9,000–₹12,000/माह।

  • हेल्पलाइन: 1800-123-9626 (Skill India)

अक्सर पूछे जाने वाले प्रश्न

शिक्षुता और इंटर्नशिप में क्या अंतर है?

NAPS शिक्षुता एक कानूनी बाध्यकारी प्रशिक्षण कार्यक्रम है जिसमें अनिवार्य वजीफा और राष्ट्रीय प्रमाण पत्र मिलता है।

NAPS पूरा होने पर क्या प्रमाण पत्र मिलता है?

सफल समापन पर NCVT द्वारा राष्ट्रीय शिक्षुता प्रमाण पत्र (NAC) मिलता है।

क्या फ्रेशर आवेदन कर सकते हैं?

हाँ, NAPS विशेष रूप से पहली बार उद्योग में व्यावहारिक अनुभव चाहने वाले युवाओं के लिए बना है।
