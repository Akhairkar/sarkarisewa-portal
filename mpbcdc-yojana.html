MPBCDC Kya Hai? (What is MPBCDC)

Mahatma Phule Backward Class Development Corporation (MPBCDC) Maharashtra Sarkar ke Social Justice and Special Assistance Department ke antargat ek mukhya sarkari sanstha hai. Iski sthapna 10 July 1978 ko hui thi. Iska mukhya uddeshya samaj ke pichhde vargon ko aarthik roop se aatmanirbhar banana aur unka utthan karna hai.

Dhyan dein: Yeh corporation SPECIFICALLY Scheduled Caste (SC) aur Neo-Buddhist community ke logon ke liye banaya gaya hai. OBC (Other Backward Classes) ya VJNT ke liye alag se corporations hain, isliye MPBCDC ki yojanaon me apply karne ke liye SC ya Neo-Buddhist caste certificate hona anivarya hai. Yeh un logon ke liye ek life-changing opportunity hai jo apna khud ka business ya self-employment shuru karna chahte hain lekin capital (paise) ki kami ke karan aage nahi badh paate.

MPBCDC apni vibhinn schemes ke madhyam se beneficiaries ko subsidy, margin money (seed capital), aur direct loans provide karta hai taaki unka financial bojh kam ho sake aur woh apne business dreams ko haqiqat me badal sakein.

Kaun Eligible Hai? (Eligibility Criteria)

In yojanaon ka labh uthane ke liye aavedak ko nimnlikhit sharton ko pura karna hota hai. Sarkar ne yeh niyam isliye banaye hain taaki sahi aur zarooratmand logon tak labh pahunch sake:

  • Resident of Maharashtra: Aavedak Maharashtra ka permanent resident (domicile holder) hona chahiye.
  • Caste Category: Aavedak Scheduled Caste (SC) ya Neo-Buddhist community ka member hona chahiye. Caste certificate anivarya hai.
  • Age Limit: Aavedak ki umar 18 se 50 saal ke beech honi chahiye. Isse bahar ke log generally apply nahi kar sakte.
  • Family Income: Parivaar ki varshik aay (Annual Income) â‚¹2.5 lakh se kam honi chahiye. (Yeh priority criteria hai taaki garib tabke ko pehle preference mile).
  • No Past Default: Person ka apna koi existing sarkari ya bank business loan default nahi hona chahiye. Pehle se kisi anya Mahamandal se loan lene wale eligible nahi hote.
  • Business Location: Jis business ya udhyog ke liye aap apply kar rahe ho, uska physical address aur sanchalan Maharashtra state me hi hona chahiye.

Zaroori Documents (Required Documents Checklist)

Aapko online portal par apply karte waqt aur baad me physical verification ke samay in sabhi dastavejon (documents) ki zaroorat padegi. Inhe pehle se scan karke PDF/JPG format me taiyar rakhein:

  • Aadhaar Card: Identity aur address proof ke liye.
  • Jati Praman Patra: Valid Caste Certificate (SC/Neo-Buddhist category) issued by Tehsildar.
  • Utpanna Praman Patra: Income Certificate (â‚¹2.5 lakh se kam ka) competent authority dwara jaari kiya hua.
  • Age Proof: School Leaving Certificate (TC), Birth Certificate ya PAN card.
  • Maharashtra Domicile Proof: Residence Certificate / Domicile certificate.
  • Bank Passbook: Kisi bhi Nationalized Bank account ki pichle 6 mahine ki statement.
  • Photos: 2 hal hi me kheenchi gayi Passport Size Photos.
  • Business Plan / Project Report: Aap kya business karna chahte hain, uski laagat, aur profit ka estimate. (Ye specially bank loan wali schemes ke liye zaroori hai).
  • Jagah ka Proof: Rent agreement, NOC ya ownership proof jahan business set up kiya jayega.

Online Kaise Apply Karein? (How to Apply)

Maharashtra sarkar ne aavedan prakriya ko poori tarah transparent aur online kar diya hai. Niche diye gaye simple steps follow karke aap MPBCDC schemes ke liye online apply kar sakte hain:

  1. Portal Par Jayein: Sabse pehle official mahadisha.in portal ya MPBCDC ki official website par visit karein.
  2. Registration: Apna 'Naya Registration' (New Registration) karein. Isme apna Aadhaar number dalein aur mobile OTP se verify karein.
  3. Form Bharein: Ek baar login karne ke baad, apni personal details, caste category, education, address, income aur apne business plan ki jankari form me bharein.
  4. Documents Upload: Sabhi zaroori documents ko clear quality me scan karke portal par (PDF/JPG format) upload karein. File size limits ka dhyan rakhein.
  5. Scheme Select Karein: Apni zaroorat aur project cost ke mutabiq scheme chunein (Direct Loan / Seed Capital / 50% Subsidy).
  6. Submit & Note Number: Form ko submit karein. Submit karne ke baad screen par ek application reference number aayega, usko zaroor note karke rakhein future tracking ke liye.
  7. Verification: MPBCDC ke District office dwara aapke documents aur application ki jaanch (verification) hogi. Isme interview ya site inspection bhi shamil ho sakta hai.
  8. Approval & Disbursement: Agar sab kuch sahi raha aur approval mil gaya, toh loan ya subsidy ka amount seedhe aapke bank account me transfer kar diya jayega (DBT ke madhyam se). Bank linked schemes me bank branch final process karti hai.
