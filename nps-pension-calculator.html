❓ अक्सर पूछे जाने वाले प्रश्न (FAQ)

NPS mein minimum contribution kitna hai?

Tier 1 account ke liye minimum contribution ₹500 per transaction aur ₹1,000 per financial year hota hai. Account active rakhne ke liye saal mein kam se kam ek baar contribute karna zaroori hai.

Tax benefit kitna milta hai?

NPS mein Sec 80CCD(1B) ke tahat ₹50,000 ka extra tax deduction milta hai, jo Sec 80C ke ₹1.5 lakh limit ke oopar hai. Saath hi employer contribution par bhi Sec 80CCD(2) ke tahat tax benefit milta hai.

Withdrawal rules kya hain aur Annuity kya hai?

60 saal ki umar par, aap total corpus ka maximum 60% lump sum tax-free withdraw kar sakte hain. Bacha hua minimum 40% hissa annuity plan kharidne mein use hota hai, jisse aapko zindagi bhar monthly pension milti hai.

National Pension System (NPS) — Complete Guide (राष्ट्रीय पेंशन प्रणाली की पूरी जानकारी)

National Pension System (NPS) ek voluntary, defined contribution retirement savings scheme hai jo PFRDA (Pension Fund Regulatory and Development Authority) dwara manage ki jaati hai. Yeh scheme retirees ko financial security pradan karne ke liye banayi gayi hai.

Contribution Rules

Government employees ke liye Tier I account mein unke basic pay aur DA ka 10% contribute hota hai, jabki government 14% contribute karti hai. Private sector employees aur general citizens apne anusaar contribute kar sakte hain (minimum ₹1000/year).

60/40 Split Rule & Tax Benefits

Retirement (60 years age) par, corpus ka 60% tax-free lump sum withdraw kiya ja sakta hai. Baaki 40% ka annuity plan kharidna compulsory hota hai jisse monthly pension milti hai. Iske alawa Section 80CCD(1B) ke antargat ₹50,000 ka extra tax deduction milta hai.
