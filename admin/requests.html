How a lead goes to a CSC

① sends the centre the task, place and these rules without the customer's number and asks it to reply "हाँ". Only after that, ② sends the name and number. ③ tells the customer which centre will call. ✅ = same district, 🟡 = same state, ⚪ = other state (only for work that needs no biometric).

    Verified CSCs: who replies

    Send each centre the lead programme message once; pause leads for centres that never reply. Centres that reply get suggested first. If a centre does not reply within 2 hours, press "✖ अगला CSC" on the request.
